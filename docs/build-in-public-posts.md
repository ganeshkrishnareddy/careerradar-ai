## LinkedIn draft

Day 3 of the OpenAI x Outskill AI Builders Hackathon: MVP checkpoint for CareerRadar AI.

I am building CareerRadar AI, an AI-powered opportunity copilot for students. The problem is simple but painful: students miss internships, jobs, hackathons, scholarships, and learning opportunities because everything is scattered across LinkedIn, job boards, college groups, newsletters, and community channels.

Today I completed the product journey, MVP scope, user flow, one-page pitch, and a clickable MVP shell.

The MVP workflow:

- Student enters profile or resume summary.
- CareerRadar AI ranks opportunities by fit.
- Each opportunity shows a fit score, eligibility, missing skills, resume gaps, prep questions, and outreach draft.
- Student saves opportunities and tracks progress.

The goal is not to build another job board. The goal is to build a career radar that tells students: why this opportunity, why you, and what to do next.

Next sprint: connect real opportunity data, add OpenAI-powered ranking, and deploy the first public MVP.
