# CareerRadar AI - One-Page Investor Pitch

## Vision

Become the opportunity intelligence layer for students and early-career talent.

## Problem

Millions of students miss internships, hackathons, scholarships, and entry-level roles because opportunity discovery is fragmented, noisy, and biased toward people who already know where to look. Existing job boards show listings, but they rarely explain fit, eligibility, skill gaps, or the next step.

## Solution

CareerRadar AI is a personalized AI career radar. A student enters their profile once, and the platform recommends relevant opportunities with fit scores, resume gaps, prep tasks, and outreach drafts.

## Product

The MVP includes:

- AI-ranked opportunities across internships, jobs, hackathons, and learning programs.
- Resume and profile fit analysis.
- Missing skill and eligibility explanations.
- Interview prep prompts for each opportunity.
- Outreach message drafts.
- Saved shortlist and progress tracking.

## Why now

AI makes it possible for one student to have a career scout, resume reviewer, and interview coach inside one workflow. Students are already using AI tools, but the workflow is scattered. CareerRadar AI packages the workflow around one urgent outcome: finding and winning better opportunities.

## Market

Initial wedge: Indian college students and early-career tech learners.

Expansion: universities, bootcamps, creator-led cohorts, placement cells, and early-career hiring communities.

## Business model

Freemium for students:

- Free: daily opportunity radar, shortlist, basic fit scores.
- Pro: resume optimization, interview prep packs, outreach automation, advanced tracking.

B2B:

- Placement-cell dashboards.
- Cohort and bootcamp opportunity intelligence.
- Recruiter-facing talent signals after student consent.

## Competitive advantage

CareerRadar AI is not just a listing aggregator. It combines personalization, preparation, and action. The long-term data advantage comes from feedback loops: what students save, skip, apply to, and win.

## Hackathon traction plan

- Day 3: clickable MVP, product brief, user flow, screenshots.
- Day 4-5: connect sample data and AI ranking.
- Launch: deploy public MVP, share demo video, collect feedback from 10-20 students.

## Ask

Looking for student beta users, mentors, and early feedback from founders, recruiters, and placement teams.
